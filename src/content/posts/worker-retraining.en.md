## Motivation

Worker retraining programs have been studied for decades, yet the literature is fragmented across fields and methodologies. This review synthesizes the strongest causal evidence published between 2005 and 2025.

## Headline finding

Across 47 randomized and quasi-experimental studies, the median program raised annual earnings by **$1,420** (2024 USD) over a 3-year horizon — a meaningful but modest effect. The 75th percentile study raised earnings by **$4,100**, driven mostly by programs that combined income support with sector-specific technical training.

## Implications

We argue that the most consistent predictor of program effectiveness is *stacking*: layering short, focused coursework with employer partnerships and a modest wage subsidy beats any single intervention on its own.
